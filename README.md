# LiberVoltis LV‑01 — Open Hardware River Turbine

LiberVoltis creates portable, reliable off‑grid energy solutions for crisis zones, field operations and remote communities.

LV‑01 is an open‑hardware micro‑hydro turbine designed to generate electricity from any flowing water.  
It is lightweight, field‑repairable and works 24/7 — no fuel, no wind, no sun.

---

## 🌍 Mission

Our mission is to provide accessible, open, and reliable energy devices for people operating in the field — from Ukraine to disaster‑affected regions.

All LiberVoltis projects are fully open‑hardware and can be built anywhere in the world.

---

## ⚡ LV‑01 Features

- continuous power generation  
- USB output  
- backpack‑friendly form factor  
- works in any weather  
- field‑repairable  
- open‑hardware design

## 📁 Repository Structure

- cad/           → mechanical design files

- docs/          → manuals, assembly, repair
- bom/           → bill of materials
- tests/         → field & lab testing
- tools/         → scripts & utilities
- media/         → renders, photos, branding
- prototypes/    → historical versions
